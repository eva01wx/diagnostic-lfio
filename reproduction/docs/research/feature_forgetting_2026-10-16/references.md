# Primary sources checked for the feature-forgetting extension

- Lin, F.; Reiter, R. (1994), *Forget It!*, AAAI Fall Symposium on Relevance,
  pp. 141–146. https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Fall/1994/FS-94-02/FS94-02-037.pdf
  Model-theoretic forgetting and retained-language consequence preservation;
  propositional substitution is classical, not a new operator of this project.
- Levy, A. Y. (1994), *Discussion of: Forget It!*, p. 147.
  https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Fall/1994/FS-94-02/FS94-02-038.pdf
  Raises representation size, inference utility and proof-dependency issues.
  We do not infer that semantic preservation supplies a derivational explanation.
- Lin, F. (2001), *On strongest necessary and weakest sufficient conditions*,
  Artificial Intelligence 128(1–2), 143–159. DOI 10.1016/S0004-3702(01)00070-4.
  Publisher metadata: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370201000704
  Author-hosted earlier version: https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/faculty/flin/papers/snc-kr00.pdf
  The two-sided projection identity and its optimal logical interpretation are
  established. This extension does not claim to originate them.
- Darwiche, A.; Hirth, A. (2020), *On the Reasons Behind Decisions*, ECAI 2020.
  Pages 712–720; DOI 10.3233/FAIA200158. https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.09284
  Sufficient, necessary and complete reasons already have a formal-XAI theory.
- Izza, Y.; Ignatiev, A.; Rubin, S.; Marques-Silva, J.; Stuckey, P. J. (2025),
  *Most General Explanations of Tree Ensembles*, IJCAI, 5463–5471.
  DOI 10.24963/ijcai.2025/608. https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/0608.pdf
  Uses minimal abductive explanations and maximizes inflated explanation coverage
  for tree ensembles. Our fixed-feature global projections are not equivalent to
  finding such a most-general instance explanation, and this comparator was not run.

All checked on 2026-10-16. Use the primary proceedings/author/publisher sources;
search-index publication ages are not used as bibliographic dates. Copyrighted
paper PDFs are linked rather than redistributed in the reproducibility archive.
